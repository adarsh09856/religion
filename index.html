About Us

Bhutan Hindu Dharma Sabha & Thimphu Hindu Temple

The Bhutan Hindu Dharma Sabha and the Central Thimphu Hindu Temple (Samteling) are dedicated to the preservation of Hindu culture, spiritual heritage and community service across Bhutan. We bring people together through faith, devotion, education and social welfare, fostering harmony in our society and strengthening the timeless values of Sanatan Dharma alongside our nation's rich traditions.
