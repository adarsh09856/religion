About Us

Bhutan Hindu Dharma Sabha & Temple

To live in a Hindu Dharma Sabha is to embrace the timeless values of Sanatana Dharma, spreading love, peace and spiritual wisdom. Our temple is a place of devotion, culture and community, where everyone is welcome to seek blessings and connect with the divine.
