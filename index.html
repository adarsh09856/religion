About Us

Bhutan Hindu Dharma Sabha & Thimphu Hindu Temple

The Bhutan Hindu Dharma Sabha and the Central Thimphu Hindu Temple (Samteling) are dedicated to the preservation of Hindu culture, spiritual heritage and community service across Bhutan. We bring people together through faith, devotion, education and social welfare, fostering harmony in our society and strengthening the values of Sanatan Dharma in peaceful co-existence.

Spiritual Sanctum Daily ritual worship, Havana, and Maha Aarti for communal well-being.
Annadanam Seva Nutritious free prasadam served daily to all visitors and the needy.
Panchang & Utsavs Accurate Vedic Bhutan panchang calculations and annual festivals.
Vedic Education Pathshala teaching Sanskrit, moral values, and cultural heritage to youth.

Together in Faith • Stronger in Community

The Bhutan Hindu Dharma Sabha is dedicated to holistic spiritual and social upliftment across all 20 Dzongkhags of Bhutan through dedicated Seva pillars.

🪔

Pujas & Daily Rituals

Daily morning and evening archana, Rudra abhishekam, Navagraha shanti, and family sankalpa pujas.

 🎉

Cultural Programs & Utsavs

Annual Dashain, Tihar, Shivaratri, Janmashtami, and communal celebrations uniting all generations.

 🍲

Annadanam & Prasadam Seva

Daily free sanctified meals served to pilgrims, monks, travelers, and the elderly in the temple hall.

 📖

Vedic Pathshala & Youth

Sanskrit shloka classes, Bhagavad Gita discourses, moral education, and cultural heritage youth camps.

 🩺

Health Camps & Welfare

Periodic free medical clinics, winter blankets distribution, and welfare support for vulnerable families.

 🤝

Community Outreach & Unity

Collaborating closely with local leadership to uphold the national vision of Gross National Happiness.
