About the Sabha & Central Temple

|| वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम् || Preserving Vedic Heritage, Community Harmony & Social Welfare in the Kingdom of Bhutan.

Our Sacred Origins & Mission

The Bhutan Hindu Dharma Sabha (BHDS) was founded to serve as the unified spiritual and socio-cultural body for the Hindu community across Bhutan. Registered under the religious organizations framework of Bhutan, the Sabha works in close collaboration with government ministries and traditional institutions to nurture peace, ethical values, and mutual respect between Bhutan's Buddhist and Hindu heritage.

The Central Thimphu Hindu Temple in Samteling was established with the noble support of His Majesty the King and the Royal Government of Bhutan. The sanctuary serves as a peaceful haven for daily prayers, national peace ceremonies, solemn rituals, and vibrant festivals such as Maha Shivaratri, Janmashtami, and Durga Puja.

🕉️

Our Divine Mission

To uphold Sanatan Dharma values, conduct authentic Vedic rituals for the welfare of the King, the Country, and all sentient beings, and support communities through Annadanam and healthcare.

🏔️

Our Shared Vision

A harmonious Bhutanese society grounded in spirituality, compassion, Vedic knowledge for the younger generation, and Gross National Happiness.

Key Pillars of Seva

The Sabha operates multiple community arms including the Samteling Priest Council, Youth Cultural Wing, and Women's Seva Samiti.

✔ Faith: Preserving orthodox Vedic temple worship & Havans
✔ Culture: Teaching Sanskrit, Bhajan, Gita & classical music
✔ Service: Daily Annadanam, health camps, elder assistance
✔ Unity: Inter-community dialogue & national integration
