About the Sabha & Central Temple

Dedicated to the preservation of ancient Sanatan Dharma, spiritual growth, cultural heritage and compassionate community service in the peaceful Kingdom of Bhutan.

Spiritual Foundation

Nurturing Harmony, Culture and Devotion in Bhutan

The Bhutan Hindu Dharma Sabha and the Central Thimphu Hindu Temple (Samteling) operate as the spiritual center for Hindu devotees across the Kingdom. Founded with Royal Blessings, the Sabha serves as a bridge uniting people through faith, devotion, moral education, and social welfare.

Under the enduring philosophy of “Dharmo Rakshati Rakshitaha” (Dharma protects those who protect it), we foster deep harmony between Hindu traditions and Bhutan's rich Buddhist national fabric, promoting mutual respect, compassionate living, and peace.

The Four Sacred Pillars of Sabha:
🕉️ Faith (Shraddha): Temple worship & pujas
🎨 Culture (Sanskriti): Music, dance & festivals
🤝 Service (Seva): Annadanam, health & charity
☸️ Unity (Ekata): National harmony & brotherhood
Compassionate Seva

Community & Welfare Initiatives

Extending spiritual devotion into selfless service for all sentient beings.

🍲

Daily Annadanam

Serving nutritious hot sattvic meals free of charge to all pilgrims, monks, travelers, and hospital patients visiting Thimphu.

🩺

Free Health Camps

Periodic mobile medical camps, eye screening clinics, and free medicine distribution organized with volunteer Bhutanese doctors.

🐄

Gaushala Sanctuary

Providing shelter, clean water, medical treatment, and lifelong care to senior and abandoned cows rescued across the valleys.

📖

Vedic Gurukul Classes

Weekend classes for youth in Sanskrit stotram chanting, Bhagavad Gita slokas, moral values, and classical Hindu cultural arts.

🤝

Interfaith Harmony

Collaborating warmly with Buddhist monasteries and national cultural bodies on auspicious royal occasions and prayer ceremonies.

❄️

Winter Blanket Drives

Distributing warm clothing, thermals, and blankets to high-altitude yak herders, rural students, and vulnerable elderly families.
