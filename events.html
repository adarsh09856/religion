Sanatan Festivals & Bhutan Panchang

Year-round spiritual observances, lunar tithi calendars, auspicious muhurats, and grand utsav celebrations at Samteling Temple, Thimphu.

2025–2026 Auspicious Festivals

Celebrated in accordance with Himalayan Vedic astronomy and local royal holidays
12 OCTOBER 2025 All-Night Vigil

Maha Shivaratri Celebration

Continuous 4-Pahar sacred Bilva Patra Abhishekam to Lord Shiva, Rudram chanting, Maha Mangala Aarti at 4:00 AM, and distribution of sanctified Mahaprasadam to 2,000+ devotees.

18 OCTOBER 2025 Midnight Darshan

Shri Krishna Janmashtami Mahotsav

Divya Janma Darshan at midnight 12:00 AM, Laddu Gopal cradle jhula seva, Vedic bhajan sandhya, traditional Bhutanese cultural dance performance, and 108 bhog offerings.

25 OCTOBER 2025 9-Day Mahotsav

Sharad Navaratri & Durga Puja

Chandi Havan, daily Durga Saptashati Parayanam, Maha Ashtami Sandhi Puja, Kanya Pujan blessing ceremony for 108 young girls, and grand Sindoor Khela on Vijayadashami.

12 NOVEMBER 2025 Deepotsav

Deepawali & Maha Lakshmi Pujan

Lighting of 10,008 earthen ghee diyas across Samteling temple mandap, Kuber Yajna, business ledger chopda pujan, and distribution of special prasad sweets.
