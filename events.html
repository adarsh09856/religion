Events, Festivals & Bhutan Panchang

|| कालो हि दुरतिक्रमः || Auspicious Utsavs, Vedic Panchang Dates, All-Night Jagrans & Community Processions.

🔱
12 OCT 2025

Maha Shivaratri Celebration & Rudra Yajna

📍 Samteling Main Sanctum ⏰ 04:00 AM - Overnight

Grand Maha Shivaratri with 4-prahar continuous Rudra Abhishekam, Bilva Patra Archana, milk offering queue, all-night Bhajan sandhya, and morning Maha Prasadam for 2,000+ devotees.

🪈
18 OCT 2025

Krishna Janmashtami Mahotsav & Jhula Utsav

📍 Temple Prayer Hall ⏰ 18:00 - 00:30 Midnight

Celebration of Bhagavan Sri Krishna's divine birth with traditional flower Jhula (cradle ceremony), Bhagavad Gita discourses, cultural dances by Pathshala children, and midnight butter offering.

🕉️
25 OCT 2025

Durga Puja & Dashain Navaratri Mahotsav

📍 Central Utsav Mandap ⏰ 9 Auspicious Days

Nine days of Devi Mahatmya (Chandi Path), Kanya Puja honoring young girls as living avatars of Maa Durga, Vijaya Dashami royal jamara & red rice tika blessings for all Bhutanese families.

🪔
05 NOV 2025

Deepawali, Maha Laxmi Puja & Govardhan Annakut

📍 Samteling Courtyard ⏰ 17:00 - 21:30

Grand festival of lights with 1,008 pure brass oil deepams illuminating the temple steps, special Dhanvantari & Laxmi Puja for business prosperity, and 56-bhog Annakut offering.

🏹
12 APR 2026

Sri Rama Navami & Akhanda Ramacharitmanas

📍 Main Temple Hall ⏰ 24-Hour Continuous Path

Non-stop 24-hour melodic chanting of the Ramacharitmanas followed by midday Janmotsav Aarti, Panchamrit snana, and public distribution of sacred Panakam and Panjiri prasadam.

🔥
02 JUN 2026

Maha Yajna for His Majesty & Peace of Bhutan

📍 Temple Yajnashala ⏰ 08:00 - 14:00

Solemn national prayer and Shanti homa conducted by 11 Vedic acharyas praying for the long life, health, and reign of the Druk Gyalpo, good harvest, and happiness of all citizens.

📰 Sabha Bulletins & Community News

TEMPLE RENOVATION UPDATE • 08 OCT 2025

Samteling Mandir Sanctum Stone Shikhara Progress

Artisans have completed the intricate hand-chiselled stone base of the central Garbhagriha. Gilded copper kalash installation will take place next month under auspicious tithi.

COMMUNITY HEALTH • 28 SEP 2025

Free Eye & General Health Camp Organized

In partnership with Thimphu general hospital volunteers, over 340 elderly devotees received complimentary spectacles, blood pressure checkups, and ayurvedic medicines.
