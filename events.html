Sacred Bhutan Hindu Festivals & Tithi Calendar

Year-round spiritual observances, auspicious muhurats, eclipse timings (Surya/Chandra Grahan), and community celebrations at Samteling Temple.

12 October 2025 Grand Utsav

Maha Shivaratri Celebration & Rudrabhishek

4-Pahar all-night vigil, continuous Bilva Patra offering, holy milk and honey Abhishekam to Lord Shiva, with devotional bhajan sandhya.

18 October 2025 Midnight Darshan

Shri Krishna Janmashtami Mahotsav

Midnight birth celebration of Lord Krishna, Laddu Gopal jhula swinging, children costume parade, and distribution of Makhan-Mishri prasadam.

25 October 2025 National Holiday

Durga Puja Navami Havan & Dashain Tika

Consecration of Jamara, Chandi Havan, Vijaya Dashami red vermilion and barley sprout blessings bestowed by elder acharyas for victory and peace.

05 November 2025 Special Yajna

Special Abhishekam, Deepavali & Lakshmi Puja

Lighting of 1,008 sacred oil diyas across the Samteling Temple grounds, Lakshmi-Ganesha Pujan for national prosperity and happiness.
