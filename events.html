Maha Shivaratri

Maha Shivaratri Celebration

All-night four-prahara Rudrabhishek, continuous bilva patra offerings, and Vedic chanting at Samteling Shiva Sanctum.

Shri Krishna Janmashtami

Janmashtami Mahotsav

Midnight birth celebration of Lord Krishna, Bhajan kirtan by youth mandali, Dahi Handi, and Makhan Bhog distribution.

Sharad Navratri

Durga Puja & Vijayadashami

9 days of Sri Chandi Parayanam, Kumkum archana, Sindoor Khela, and grand Vijayadashami Tika blessings with elders.

📍 Samteling Temple Sanctum | All Day
