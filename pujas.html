Available Temple Rituals

Samagri, sacred flowers, gangajal and pure ghee are provided by the temple.

🔱

Maha Rudrabhishek

Dakshina: Nu. 2,100

Sacred Vedic bathing of Bhagwan Shiva with panchamrit, sugarcane juice, honey and continuous chanting of the powerful Sri Rudram Namakam and Chamakam.

🦁

Maa Durga Chandi Havan

Dakshina: Nu. 3,500

Grand sacrificial fire ritual invoking Maa Durga's supreme blessings for overcoming legal challenges, negative energies, disease and family protection.

🪷

Shri Satyanarayan Vrat Katha

Dakshina: Nu. 1,500

Traditional Purnima katha recitation invoking Lord Vishnu for peace, harmonious family relations, auspicious beginnings and prosperous homesteads.

☀️

Navgraha Shanti & Dosha Nivaran

Dakshina: Nu. 2,500

Pacification of the nine celestial planetary deities with specific samidha woods, planetary mantras, and colored cloth offerings according to your Janam Kundali.

🚗

Vehicle / Vahan Blessing Puja

Dakshina: Nu. 501

Consecration of new and commercial vehicles outside temple gates with Ganesha stotram, lemon tire crushing, swastika vermilion and safe journey raksha sutra.

🚩

Sundarkand Path & Hanuman Chalisa

Dakshina: Nu. 1,100

Complete rhythmic recitation of Valmiki / Tulsidas Sundarkand invoking Sankatmochan Hanuman for courage, mental strength and overcoming planetary afflictions.
