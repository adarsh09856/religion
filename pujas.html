Vedic Pujas, Homams & Sanskars

Conducted strictly according to Vedic Shastras by accredited Hindu priests at the Samteling Temple Sanctum or at your private residence in Bhutan.

Sacred Puja Directory

All samagri (holy ritual ingredients) arranged by Samteling Temple Seva committee
🔱

Maha Rudrabhishek with Namakam & Chamakam

Holy bath of the sacred Shiva Lingam with 11 holy substances (milk, curd, honey, ghee, sugarcane, gangajal, bilva leaves) accompanied by Sri Rudram Vedic recitations for health and liberation.

Duration: 2.5 Hours • Dakshina: Nu. 2,100
🚗

Vahan Puja (Vehicle Consecration Blessing)

Consecration for cars, Hilux 4x4s, taxis and heavy trucks for safe mountain journeys across Dochula, Chelela, and Thimphu highways with coconut breaking and Raksha Sutra tying.

Duration: 30 Mins • Dakshina: Nu. 501
📖

Shri Satyanarayan Vrat Katha & Havan

Auspicious full moon (Purnima) or house blessing ritual reciting Lord Vishnu's divine glory. Concludes with Panchamrit preparation and pure ghee Havan fire ritual.

Duration: 2 Hours • Dakshina: Nu. 1,501
🛡️

Durga Saptashati & Chandi Havan

Potent 700-verse recitation invoking Goddess Mahadurga for overcoming insurmountable obstacles, legal victory, warding off negative energies, and granting spiritual courage.

Duration: 4 Hours • Dakshina: Nu. 3,501
🪐

Navagraha Shanti & Dosha Nivarana

Pacification of 9 planetary deities (Surya, Chandra, Mangala, Budha, Guru, Shukra, Shani, Rahu, Ketu) with designated samidha woods, grains, and Vedic mantra ahutis.

Duration: 3 Hours • Dakshina: Nu. 2,501
🏡

Griha Pravesh & Vastu Shanti

Housewarming ritual for new apartments or residences across Thimphu and Paro. Includes Gau Puja, milk boiling ceremony, Vastu Purusha invocation, and threshold Raksha.

Duration: 3.5 Hours • Dakshina: Nu. 3,101
