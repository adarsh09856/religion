Vedic Pujas, Homams & Sanskar Rites

Conducted strictly according to Shastric injunctions by certified Acharyas at Samteling Temple or at your private residence in Bhutan.

🔱

Maha Rudrabhishek with Namakam & Chamakam

Holy bath of the sacred Shiva Lingam with 11 holy liquids (milk, curd, honey, ghee, sugarcane juice, coconut water, bilva leaves) for peace, removal of karmic afflictions, and health.

Duration: 2.5 Hours • Dakshina: Nu. 2,100
🚗

Vahan Puja (New Vehicle Consecration)

Consecration of new or existing cars, trucks, and taxis with turmeric, kumkum, sacred thread, coconut breaking, and 4-lemon tire blessing for safe journey across Himalayan roads.

Duration: 30 Mins • Dakshina: Nu. 501
📖

Shri Satyanarayan Vrat Katha

Auspicious recitation of Lord Vishnu's glory on Purnima (full moon) or special family milestones for domestic tranquility, harmony, and prosperity.

Duration: 2 Hours • Dakshina: Nu. 1,501
