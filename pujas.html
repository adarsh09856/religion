Sacred Temple Pujas & Havans

|| यज्ञाङ्ग रूपो भगवान् || Authentic Vedic Rituals Performed by Head Acharyas at Samteling Mandir for Family Peace & Prosperity.

🔱

Maha Rudra Abhishekam

Elaborate holy abhishekam of the sacred Shivalingam with 11 Dravyas (milk, curd, honey, ghee, sugarcane juice, sacred water) with Sri Rudram chant for health and inner peace.

Nu. 1,100
🔥

Maha Mrityunjaya Homa

Vedic fire ritual with 1,008 recitations of the Maha Mrityunjaya mantra seeking longevity, recovery from ailments, protection from untimely dangers, and universal peace.

Nu. 2,500
🪐

Navagraha Shanti Havan

Propitiation of the nine planetary deities to neutralize astrological planetary afflictions (Graha Dosha), invite success in career/education, and restore domestic harmony.

Nu. 1,500
🪔

Sri Satyanarayan Vrat Katha

Purnima or special occasion katha honoring Bhagavan Vishnu, recitation of the five divine chapters, banana leaf mandap puja, and consecrated panchamrit & halwa distribution.

Nu. 800
⚔️

Chandi Path & Durga Yajna

Sacred recitation of Durga Saptashati chapters invoked with ahutis of ghee, black sesame, kheer, and sweet meats for removal of insurmountable obstacles and negative energies.

Nu. 2,100
☀️

Gayatri Maha Yajna & Upanayana

Solemn Vedic initiation and wisdom fire ceremony purifying the intellect (Buddhi), enhancing memory and focus for students, and conferring sacred Gayatri blessings.

Nu. 1,800
