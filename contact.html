Temple Etiquette & Guidelines

  • 👞 Footwear: Please deposit shoes and leather belts at the designated shoe counter before ascending temple steps.
  • 👔 Respectful Attire: Modest clothing covering shoulders and knees is mandatory. Traditional Gho/Kira or Kurta/Sari is encouraged.
  • 📷 Photography: Photography is permitted in the outer courtyards. Photography inside the inner Garbhagriha (Sanctum Sanctorum) is prohibited.
  • 🍏 Offerings: Fresh flowers, fruits, and pure ghee may be handed to the on-duty priest for deity archana and sanctification.
Visiting Hours: Sanctum opens daily from 5:30 AM to 12:30 PM, and re-opens from 4:30 PM to 8:45 PM for Sandhya Aarti.
