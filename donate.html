Sacred Seva & Dharma Offerings

Support daily temple rituals, Nitya Annadanam meals, indigenous Gaushala cattle care, and Hindu cultural preservation across Bhutan.

🍚

Nitya Annadanam (Prasadam Seva)

Provide sanctified vegetarian meals to 300+ daily pilgrims, sadhus and monks visiting Thimphu Temple. Sponsor in honour of family birthdays or pitru remembrance.

Nu. 5,001 / Day Meal Sponsorship

Gaushala & Indigenous Cow Welfare

Shelter, organic mountain grass fodder, clean water and veterinary treatment for 75+ indigenous Himalayan cattle housed at the Temple Gaushala.
